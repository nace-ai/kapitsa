# Re-runs, 2026-10-25/26: MacBook Pro M5 Max, 128 GB

The runs after the [first pass](README.md) of this session, and the cells the README poster takes from them. Same Mac,
builds, weights and harness as there (`compare_engines.py configs/*.json --only <config> --skip tools,agent`, one config
at a time under the GPU lock); the published file's [erratum](README.md#erratum-2026-10-26) lists what they supersede.

**Power.** The first pass and the re-runs up to 08:27 ran on AC power in automatic mode. From 08:44 to 09:12 the Mac ran
on battery (High Power mode), and from about 12:55 on AC in High Power mode (`powermode 2`; Low Power Mode was off). The
*Power* column gives each run's state as the harness recorded it at the run's start. No current cell comes from a
battery run. Every cell of a row is in one power mode: the 27B and Bolt rows in High Power mode, the 35B-A3B row in
automatic mode.

**The 27B row: one block.** Kapitsa's and Splash's 27B cells were close in single AC runs (Kapitsa 146.7 and 138.3,
Splash 115.0 and 130.5 tok/s at T = 0.6), inside either engine's run-to-run spread. Decided before running: the whole
row as one block under one GPU lock, Kapitsa and Splash first in turn (K S K S K S), each of their cells the median of
its three, then every other 27B config once. The block waited for a quiet GPU and a quiet CPU (1-minute load under 6)
and ran 20:44-21:41 on AC in High Power mode; the desktop app's GPU load stayed at 0.5% or less in every phase and no
run carries a contention flag. Kapitsa's median (133.2) and mlx-serve DFlash2's single run (133.0) came out 0.15% apart, a tie.
mlx-serve then ran twice more in the same conditions (22:02, 22:04), an hour after Kapitsa's last run and not
interleaved with it; its cell is the median of its three, 127.9, and the row is reported as a tie between the two.
