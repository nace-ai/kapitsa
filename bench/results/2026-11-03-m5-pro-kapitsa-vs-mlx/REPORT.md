## How to read the numbers

- **valid**: the leg passed every check: AC power proven by a 30-second power log, no harness flag, foreign GPU load 3% or less.
- **\***: a clean-phase reading. The leg as a whole was contended (WebKit of the DSH app, Slack/Edge/Kapitan helpers, 5-16% foreign GPU load in some phase), but this phase read 3% or less. Median over the legs with that clean phase.
- **(n)**: contended reading only (every phase of every AC leg was above 3% foreign GPU load); shown for scale, not for a claim.
- **–**: no usable reading yet. "cannot measure" means the legs voided under the rule (one retry after 10 min) because of foreign GPU load from open apps.

## Flash-Next 180B Q2_0 (the poster row)

- The README row runs `--engine splash` with KFlash on Splash 1.5.0; none of #887, #890, #892 or #897 touch that path.
- Native Flash-Next verify attention is a sparse top-k kernel (`attn_rows_sparse`, 12 full-attention layers, 2048-key budget). It is 3.2% of a decode cycle at 32K and 3.0% at 128K (37-step GPU profile, M5 Pro), so a matrix-unit port could gain about 2% at best. Not built.
- Old build `71826a86` against new `a2f42108`, native Flash-Next, interleaved A/B, n=3 at 32K and 128K and n=2 at 4K (one 4K run lost to a GPU out-of-memory error; see `bench/results/2026-11-03-m5-pro-flash-next-attn-ab/`): decode median 4K +3.1% (n=2, pairs +1.1 to +5.1%), 32K -5.8% (pairs -12.3 to -3.2%), 128K +7.0% (pairs +1.9 to +7.0%); prefill -2.0%, -1.5%, -5.2%. The sign changes with context length, the diff does not touch the native Flash-Next verify path, and the Mac sat on 5-10 GB of swap, so read it as run-to-run drift (about the same), not a speedup or a regression.
- MLX cannot run Flash-Next on this Mac: the 4-bit pack is about 100 GB and the 2-bit about 81 GB against 48 GB of memory and 92 GiB of free disk.

## What #892 changed, in plain words

#892 moved the 35B-A3B (and Clover) draft-verify attention onto the M5 matrix units. It makes decoding at very long contexts faster on the Max (35B: 97 to 135 tokens per second at 249K tokens) and has no effect at 4K to 32K or on the poster row. It does not apply to Flash-Next: that model attends over at most 2,048 selected keys, so its attention was never the slow part.
