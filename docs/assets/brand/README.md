# Kapitsa press kit

Kapitsa is a project by [Nace AI](https://nace.ai).

The mark draws the Kapitza–Dirac effect, which Pyotr Kapitsa and Paul Dirac predicted in 1933: a beam of electrons
that crosses a standing wave of light comes out as several beams. A beam comes in from the left, four fringes of the
standing wave form the K's stem, and three beams leave it: the middle one straight on, the outer two as the K's arms.
One step of speculative decoding does the same with tokens.

## Type

The wordmark is set in Geist Mono SemiBold (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and converted to outlines. Text beside the
logo can use Geist Mono or any monospaced face.

## Clear space and size

Keep clear space around the mark and the logo at least half the mark's height on every side. Use the mark at 24 px
tall or more, and the logo at 120 px wide or more.

## Please don't

- Recolor the mark, or put the dark-background versions on light backgrounds (and the other way round).
- Stretch, skew, rotate or crop it, or rearrange the beams and the fringes.
- Add effects: shadows, glows, outlines or gradients beyond the beam's own.
